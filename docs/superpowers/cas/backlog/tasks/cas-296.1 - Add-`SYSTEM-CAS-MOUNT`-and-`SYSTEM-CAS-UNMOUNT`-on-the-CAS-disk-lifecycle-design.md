## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
`UNMOUNT` stops background work, releases the mount lease and ejects the disk; `MOUNT` opens the pool without needing a table.
CAS-149 designs that lifecycle (today disks are never torn down on `DROP TABLE`). The verbs are its operator surface.

Provenance: umbrella-roadmap.md section 7 bullet '`SYSTEM CAS ...` commands' and section 4 'Disk lifecycle'; filed 2026-09-26 (user decision). Verified 2026-09-26 against c16a2589f56 (cas-gc-rebuild).
