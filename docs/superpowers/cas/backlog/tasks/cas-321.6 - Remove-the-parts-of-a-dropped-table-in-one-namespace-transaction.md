## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
RC3 of the CAS-223 RCA: DROP of a 3-part table runs 19 ref-log transactions, each a _log chunk plus a _ckpt rewrite, plus 6 manifests and one catalog write, serially, each append preceded by a catalog GET; one cycle costs 83 GET + 64 PUT on CAS vs 12 + 31 on plain S3. Collapse the per-part repoints and removals of one DROP (and of a part-removal batch) into one transaction per namespace, and elide the delete_tmp_* repoint when the part is removed at once (CAS-83, CAS-286).
