## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A fetched part is committed as a whole-part CAS transaction under its temporary name (`DataPartsExchange.cpp:1307` at antalya-26.6 @e2dd0f5d343), and the later `renameParts` -> `renameTo` -> `moveDirectory` republishes it under the final name: 2 manifest PUTs and 5 ref-log appends per fetched part, of which 1 PUT and 3 appends are avoidable. Inserts and merges avoid this: they `precommitTransaction` and let `MergeTreeData::Transaction::renameParts` commit after the rename (`MergeTreeData.cpp:9407-9413`).
Cut: keep the fetch's transaction open until `renameParts`, as inserts do. Read from the code, not measured: count `build_publish` on `tmp-fetch_*` refs in `system.cas_log` on a replicated stand first.
Source: `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-09-30-cas-part-removal-flow/02-design-inputs.md`, part B.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
